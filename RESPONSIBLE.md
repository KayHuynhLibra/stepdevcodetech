## 1. Virtual points only

In-app **xu** are entertainment points with **no cash value** and **no cash-out** through the Service. Wins and losses of xu are not income, investment results, or real-money gambling outcomes.

## 2. Time & wellbeing

Take breaks. If play causes stress, sleep loss, or interferes with work/study/relationships, stop and sign out. The Service is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, or financial help.

## 3. Fortune-telling modules

Tarot / oracle / astrology-style features are **entertainment only**. Do not make important life, health, legal, or money decisions based solely on draws.

## 4. Age

The Service is **18+**. Do not allow children to use your account. Contact Feedback to request deletion if a minor’s account is discovered.

## 5. Help resources

If you or someone you know has a problem with **real-money** gambling (separate from virtual xu), in the United States you may contact the National Problem Gambling Helpline: **1-800-522-4700** or visit [ncpgambling.org](https://www.ncpgambling.org/).  
For in-app abuse or harassment, use Feedback / report tools.
